The Alignment Veto

When an aligned LLM refuses a culturally sensitive question, it often still knows the answer. Its hidden logits match real human survey data better than its own spoken answers do. We call this the alignment veto.

Key findings

What we found

The veto

Refusals hide more accurate answers

We read the logits behind each refusal (EV-NVAS) and compare them with what the model says when it answers (NVAS).

0.718 vs 0.690

refused vs. answered (p < 10−13) · 19 of 23 models

Native language backfires

In Arabic, 14 countries become one

In English, each country gets its own answers

66.5% identical answers across the 14 Arabic-speaking countries (vs 46.5% in English).

Interpretability

The gate is causal

Preference tuning may be what installs it.

  • Refusal direction · steering takes refusals from 0% to 100%
  • Activation patching · 42 of 48 refusals flip to answers
  • Sparse autoencoder · one feature fires only on sensitive questions; turning it off shifts answers by +0.29
